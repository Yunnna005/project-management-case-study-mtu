# Korean Culture Festival — Project & Financial Planning Case Study

A full project management case study for a hypothetical annual Korean Culture Festival in Dublin, built around a complete budget, financial risk analysis, and procurement strategy. Completed individually for a Software Project Management module, applying the project management framework from Kathy Schwalbe's *Information Technology Project Management*.

## My Role

**Project Manager** — I owned the project end-to-end: business case, budget, scheduling, risk, procurement strategy, and stakeholder communications, as if reporting to a real sponsor (the Korean Embassy in Dublin).

## Skills Applied

Although the subject is a cultural event rather than a company.

- **Budgeting & cost estimation** — built a full project budget (€11,528 total) from the ground up across 8 workstreams, with a 10% contingency reserve sized and justified against project risk
- **Contract & procurement strategy** — compared Cost, Firm Fixed Price, and Unit Price contract structures for each major spend category and justified which to use where, based on risk transfer and cost certainty
- **Risk analysis** — built a probability × impact risk register covering financial, schedule, and delivery risk, with a mitigation and response plan for each
- **Financial reporting & close-out** — designed the post-event financial reporting and sponsor reporting process
- **Stakeholder & governance structure** — designed a matrix organisation and a communications plan defining reporting lines up to a sponsor (Embassy) and steering committee, similar to reporting into a board or investment committee

## Methodology

- **Framework:** Kathy Schwalbe's project management methodology (PMBOK-aligned)
- **Life cycle:** Predictive (waterfall) — justified over an Agile/adaptive approach, since scope, budget, and delivery date were fixed and known upfront
- **Organisational structure:** Strong matrix — Project Manager holds overall authority; functional Team Leads (Logistics, Marketing, Finance & Admin, Culture Programming) contribute resources and expertise
